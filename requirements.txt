NOTES FOR ENUMERATOR/ ????????? ?? ??? ?????:DEFINITION OF HOUSEHOLD AND RESPONDENTS (REQUIRED)?????? ?? ???????????? ?? ??????? (????????)A household is a group of people who live together.  In our survey, a household member is someone who has lived in the household for at least 6 months in the last 1 year.  Even those persons who are not related by blood (such as servants, lodgers, or agricultural laborers) are members of the household if they have stayed in the household for at least 3 months of the past 6 months. It must be noted that someone who stays in the same household but does not bear any costs for food or does not take food from the same pot will not be considered as household member.  For example, if two brothers stay in the same house with their families but do not share food costs and cook separately, then they are considered two separate households.  Generally, if one person stays more than 3 months out of the last 6 months outside the household, they are not considered household members. We do not include them even if other household members consider them as household members. ?? ?????? ?? ??? ???? ???? ????? ?? ?? ???? ???  ????? ????????? ???, ?? ?? ?? ????? ?? ?? ?? ?? ?? ?? 6 ????? ?? ??? ??? ???  ???? ?? ?? ?? ??????? ?? ???? ????? ???? ???
